## ✦ What is LOFIII?

LOFIII brings your Lo-Fi music experience into one clean, distraction-free app. Stream or download your favorite tracks, build playlists, and tune the sound exactly how you like — all without paying a cent or seeing a single ad.

<br/>

## 🎛️ Features

**Streaming & Library**
— Lo-Fi music library across genres and artists, streamable on demand
— Full offline support — download at 320kbps and listen anywhere
— Play history, favorites, and smart playlist management

**Search & Discovery**
— Search by song, album, artist, or playlist instantly

**Audio & Playback**
— Lyrics support, inbuilt equalizer, and cache for fast loading
— Background playback with lock screen controls

**Personalization**
— Dark mode, custom accent colors, gradients, and theme options

**Honest by design**
— No ads. No subscriptions. No tracking.

## 🛠️ Installation

Download the latest APK from the [Releases page](https://github.com/ffurqanuddin/lofiii/releases) and install it on any Android device.

> Requires Flutter installed locally if building from source. Run `flutter pub get` then `flutter run`.
